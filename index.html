About Us

Welcome to Hotel Tashidelek, your authentic Nepali dining destination in Charikot, Dolakha. We take pride in serving traditional Nepali cuisine that captures the true essence of our rich culinary heritage.

Our restaurant specializes in authentic dhindo, delicious khana sets, handmade momos, flavorful chowmein, aromatic sukuti, and crispy potato fries. Every dish is prepared with fresh, local ingredients and traditional cooking methods passed down through generations.

Experience the warmth of Nepali hospitality in our cozy atmosphere, where every meal is a celebration of flavor and tradition. Join us for an unforgettable culinary journey through the tastes of Nepal.
